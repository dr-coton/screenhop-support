ScreenHop Privacy Policy

Effective date: [To be confirmed — release date] · Version 1.0

Code Black Inc. (“we”) handles your information in the iPad app ScreenHop and the Mac helper ScreenHop Host (together, “the apps”) as described below.

1. Personal information we collect: none

  • The apps do not collect personal information and send nothing to our servers. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and we run no relay servers.
  • The apps use no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. They do not show an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Your Mac’s screen contents, keyboard, mouse and touch input, clipboard, sound and SSH terminal contents travel encrypted only between your iPad and your Mac (or the SSH server you specify). We do not collect them on our servers and do not permanently record session contents to files (except device memory used to display the screen, and content you yourself copy and paste).

This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes its data (Keychain items may remain under iPadOS policy; you can remove a paired Mac’s trust key with Forget under Settings › Paired Macs, SSH passwords by deleting the host, and SSH private keys under Terminal › Key management).

3. Local network

The apps ask for the iPadOS Local Network permission to find Macs (and SSH machines) on the same Wi-Fi. Information about discovered devices is used only on your device to show the list.

4. Children

The apps are not directed to children and do not collect children’s information.

5. Contact

Because we hold no information that can identify you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete on request.
