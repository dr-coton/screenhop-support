ScreenHop Host for Mac

The free helper that lets the ScreenHop app on your iPad use this Mac. It runs in the menu bar and collects no personal information.

Coming soon

The ScreenHop Host download is being prepared. The download button will appear here when it is ready.

Requirements

  • macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), with a user logged in
  • The ScreenHop app on your iPad (App Store link after release)
  • The same Wi-Fi. Away from home you can set up a VPN such as Tailscale yourself and type the Mac’s address into the iPad. USB cable connection is not supported.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Open the downloaded ScreenHop-Host-version.dmg.
  2. Drag ScreenHop Host into the Applications folder.
  3. Open ScreenHop Host from Applications and follow the prompts to allow Screen Recording and Accessibility. After you allow them, macOS may ask you to reopen the app.

ScreenHop Host runs in the menu bar (you may not see a Dock window). Turn on “Open at login” in its menu to have it ready whenever the Mac starts.

The 2 permissions

PermissionWhat it is for
Screen RecordingTo show this Mac’s screen on the iPad.
AccessibilityTo control the mouse and keyboard from the iPad.

You can turn either on or off at any time under System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Connecting the first time

  1. On the same Wi-Fi, open ScreenHop on the iPad and tap your Mac in the list.
  2. Check that the iPad and the Mac show the same 6-digit code.
  3. Click Allow in the Mac’s menu bar. After that it connects automatically.

If the Mac is locked, unlock it at the Mac itself. If it won’t connect, see Troubleshooting.

Privacy

Your screen, keystrokes and clipboard go encrypted over the local network to your paired iPad only. Nothing is sent to our servers, and there are no analytics or ad SDKs. Privacy policy

Removing it

  1. Before deleting, open the ScreenHop Host menu in the menu bar › Paired iPads and choose Remove for your iPad (this deletes the pairing key; that iPad would need to pair again).
  2. Choose Quit ScreenHop Host in the menu bar.
  3. Move ScreenHop Host from the Applications folder to the Trash.
  4. Under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording and Accessibility, turn off or remove the ScreenHop Host entries.

Updates

There is no automatic update for now. Download the new version from this page and install it over the app in Applications. When an update changes the connection protocol, it ships the same day as the iPad app, and this page will say “requires iPad app x.y or later” if needed.
